    Glow Secrets From the Salon

    Three complimentary tips below. Members unlock the full library of 25 professional tips inside Luminate Glow — updated with 2026 New Sunshine guidance.

    01

    Exfoliate 24–48 Hours Before — Not the Day Of

    Exfoliating immediately before a session removes the fresh skin surface and can cause uneven color absorption and increased sensitivity in the bed. Exfoliate 24–48 hours prior to allow the skin to settle, then arrive clean and product-free for the best color result.

    🛏️ Bed + Spray Tan
    02

    Prep Dry Areas Before Every Spray Session

    Knees, elbows, ankles, and the backs of hands have thicker, drier skin that absorbs spray solution disproportionately — leading to darker patches and faster fading in those areas. Apply a light, fragrance-free, water-based moisturizer to those spots only before stepping into the booth. Avoid applying to the rest of the body, as it will block color absorption.

    ✨ Spray Tan
    03

    Frequency Over Duration for Red Light Therapy

    Red light therapy is a cumulative treatment — the skin responds to consistent, repeated exposure at the cellular level rather than single extended sessions. Four to five shorter sessions per week produce significantly better results for collagen production, mood, and skin quality than one long session weekly. Consistency is the protocol.

    Understanding Lotion Types

    Selecting the correct lotion type for your service and skin goal is the foundation of an effective tanning routine. Each type serves a distinct purpose and is formulated differently.

    Intensifiers

    White-based lotions that work through hydration and skin conditioning to enhance the skin's natural color potential. Contain Tyrosine, which stimulates melanin production, allowing the skin to develop its darkest natural pigment. No added color — all results are the skin's own response. Recommended for beginners building a base tan and for tanners who prefer a natural result.

    Natural Bronzers

    Enhance the tanning process by depositing immediate color through natural ingredients such as Caramel, Henna, Annatto, Black Walnut Shell, and Tyrosine. Designed to encourage color development and establish a base tan. Results are streak-free and stain-free. Color is cosmetic and will gradually fade — not a replacement for a UV-developed tan. Ideal for clients seeking visible, same-session results without DHA.

    Delayed Bronzers (DHA)

    Utilize DHA (dihydroxyacetone) and often Erythrulose — both sugar cane extracts — which chemically darken the protein in the outer layer of the skin. Color develops 2–4 hours post-session and lasts 4–7 days. The higher the DHA concentration, the darker the result. Hands must be washed immediately after application to prevent buildup. Do not shower for a minimum of 4 hours after tanning to allow full color development.

    Tingles & Sizzles ⚠️ Experienced Tanners Only

    Contain Benzyl Nicotinate, which increases microcirculation in the skin, producing a warming, reddening sensation that drives deeper color development. Recommended exclusively for experienced tanners with an established base. A sample packet must be tried prior to purchasing a full-size bottle. Never apply to the face. Tanning bed must be cleaned twice following any tingle lotion use. Not recommended for sensitive skin.

    Double & Triple Dip

    Specifically formulated for clients moving between UV tanning and spray or red light services in the same session. Standard lotions contain silicone, which blocks pores and prevents the skin from receiving spray booth color or absorbing red light rays — resulting in splotchy, uneven, or absent color. Only products labeled as Double or Triple Dip should be used when combining services. This is a formulation difference, not a preference.

    Plateau Breakers / Optimizers

    A tanning plateau occurs when progress stalls after a period of consistent activity. Optimizer formulas contain the highest concentration of instant and delayed bronzers, along with ingredients such as CuO2 and TRF, designed to break through color plateaus and re-stimulate results. For use 1–2 times per week only — not for daily sessions. Best suited for experienced tanners who have maintained a consistent routine and are no longer seeing color advancement.

    DermaDark™

    Builds gradual color between sessions without UV exposure. Found in tan extenders — doubles as a daily moisturizer with color benefit.

    Silicone Base

    Allows lotion to glide on evenly. Produces a matte, non-greasy finish. Lightweight consistency for quick application. Not compatible with spray tan or red light services.

    Pu(red)™ Technology

    California Tan exclusive. Allows the purest red light wavelengths to penetrate the skin without obstruction — essential for maximizing red light therapy results.

    ATO Inhibitors

    After Tan Odor fighters are found in nearly every professional lotion. Neutralize post-session odor caused by the UV oxidation of skin proteins.

    Preparation Is the Foundation of Every Successful Spray Tan

    The quality of a spray tan result is largely determined before the client enters the booth. Proper exfoliation 24–48 hours prior removes the dry, uneven surface layer that causes premature fading and patchiness. Areas with thicker skin — knees, elbows, and ankles — require additional attention, as they absorb color disproportionately without targeted prep. Fragrance-free, water-based moisturizers are appropriate for dry spot prepping immediately before service. Oil-based products and deodorant should be avoided before a spray session.

    How Red Light Therapy Lotion Works Differently

    Red light therapy operates on a fundamentally different mechanism than UV tanning. Rather than stimulating melanin production, red light penetrates the skin at the cellular level to support collagen and elastin production, improve circulation, and deliver anti-aging and wellness benefits. Standard tanning lotions containing silicone create a barrier on the skin's surface that restricts red light penetration, reducing session effectiveness. Lotions formulated for red light — particularly those featuring Pu(red)™ Technology by California Tan — are designed to allow optimal red light wavelength transmission without obstruction. Collagen-boosting and peptide-rich formulas specifically enhance the skin's response to red light treatment.

    Why Lotion Selection Is Critical for Combination Services

    Standard tanning lotions are formulated with silicone, which forms a barrier on the surface of the skin. When a client uses a non-dip lotion and proceeds to a spray tan booth or red light booth, this barrier prevents the spray color from absorbing evenly and blocks red light rays from reaching the skin effectively. The result is splotching, streaking, uneven color development, or no results from red light. This is a formulation science issue, not a client error — the lotion must be specifically labeled and designed as a Double or Triple Dip product for combination services to function correctly.

    ✦ From Isabella's Desk

    What Clients Always Get Wrong

    ☀️

    "I shower right after tanning so I don't smell"

    This is one of the most common things I heard. If you're using a DHA bronzer, showering within 4 hours is literally washing your tan down the drain before it finishes developing. The smell is what ATO inhibitors in your lotion are for — not an early shower.

    🛏️

    "I don't need lotion, I tan fine without it"

    Dry skin reflects UV light. Without lotion you need significantly longer sessions to achieve the same color — which means more UV exposure, more cost, and results that fade faster. You're not saving money. You're spending more time in the bed for worse results.

    ✨

    "I did bed tanning before my spray and used my regular lotion"

    Regular lotions contain silicone. Silicone blocks pores. The spray solution cannot absorb evenly into blocked pores — which is why you get splotchy, streaky results and blame the spray tech when it's actually a lotion chemistry issue. You need a Double or Triple Dip formula. Every time. No exceptions.

    💡 Tanning Myth Busting

    Things the Industry
    Wants You to Believe

    Let's clear these up once and for all.

    MYTH

    A base tan protects you from burning

    A base tan provides minimal UV protection — roughly SPF 3. It is not sunscreen. Knowing your Fitzpatrick skin type and managing session time is what actually prevents burns.

    MYTH

    Higher level beds always give better results

    Bed level affects the UV spectrum and intensity — not just "more UV." The right bed for you depends on your Fitzpatrick type, your base tan, and your goal. More isn't always better.

    MYTH

    Tingle lotions are just stronger bronzers

    Tingle contains Benzyl Nicotinate — a vasodilator that increases microcirculation. It's not about bronzers at all. That's why the redness and heat happens, and why it's not appropriate for all skin types.

    MYTH

    Red light therapy gives you a tan

    Red light does not produce melanin and does not tan the skin. It works at the cellular level — supporting collagen, reducing inflammation, and improving energy and mood. It complements tanning but is a completely separate mechanism.

    MYTH

    Expensive lotions are just a sales tactic

    Sometimes. But the price difference between a $15 and $65 lotion is often real — higher DHA concentration, multiple bronzer layers, collagen peptides, Pu(red) technology. The Luminate Glow lotion guide breaks down exactly what you're paying for in every tier.

    MYTH

    You have to tan every day to maintain color

    Daily tanning is unnecessary and counterproductive. Your skin needs 24–48 hours to complete the melanin production cycle after a session. Using a DermaDark™ tan extender daily at home maintains and builds color between sessions without any extra UV.
